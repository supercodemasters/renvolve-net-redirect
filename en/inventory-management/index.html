Inventory management with the right stock level

Stock should match in the warehouse, in the web shop and in the books at the same time.

Wrong stock levels lead to back orders, needless purchases and money sitting on the shelf. An inventory system counts stock from real events: goods receipt, sales, shrinkage and stocktaking. Then everyone sees the same number.

What it can include

  • Stock per item, location and warehouse
  • Goods receipt with cost per batch, including freight
  • Shrinkage, write-offs and stocktaking
  • Reorder points that warn before stock runs out

Relevant experience

  • Inventory and goods receipt with cost tracking, shrinkage, packages and freight costs (Samsara Solutions, Bulgaria)
  • Inventory control for a shipyard, on mobile too (Uzmar, Turkey)
  • Stock and price sync with a web shop (PopLeads, Turkey)

Frequently asked questions

Can inventory sync with our web shop?

Yes. Stock and prices can be updated automatically in both directions.

Does it work with barcodes?

Yes. Goods receipt and picking can be done with a phone that reads barcodes.
