Services

Orders and invoicing without double work

An order should only be entered once, wherever it comes from.

Orders arrive by phone, email, web shop and sales staff. If every channel is handled separately, invoices end up late and wrong. A shared order flow gathers all orders, shows status and creates the invoice basis automatically.

What can be built

  • A quote that becomes an order in one click
  • Order status that both you and the customer can see
  • Invoice basis straight from the delivered order
  • Reminders for unpaid invoices

Good to know

  • The invoice can be created in your existing finance system via a connection
  • VAT and account coding follow your rules
  • Everything is logged so the auditor can follow the chain

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to change finance system?

No. The order flow is usually connected to the finance system you already have.

Can customers see their orders?

Yes, in a customer portal with order status, deliveries and invoices.
