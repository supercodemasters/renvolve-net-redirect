Purchasing and suppliers in one flow

From need to order, goods receipt and invoice without email chains.

When purchasing runs on email and spreadsheets, it is hard to see what has been ordered, who approved it and what it cost. A purchasing flow gathers the order, the approval and the supplier in one place and links it to inventory and bookkeeping.

Common problems

  • Nobody knows for sure what has already been ordered
  • Approvals happen in emails that are hard to find later
  • Supplier details live in several different lists

What can be built

  • Purchase orders with approval in one or more steps
  • A supplier register with contracts and contacts
  • Goods receipt against the order, with deviations
  • A supplier portal where suppliers upload documents themselves

Relevant experience

  • Supplier portal for 500+ suppliers with roles and permissions (ITG, Turkey)
  • Purchase orders and inventory in an ERP system (Samsara Solutions, Bulgaria)

Frequently asked questions

Can suppliers log in themselves?

Yes. A supplier portal lets them update details, upload certificates and see their orders.

How many approval levels can there be?

As many as you need, for example by amount or cost centre.
