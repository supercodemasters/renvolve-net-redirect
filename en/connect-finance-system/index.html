Services

Connect the business to the finance system

You rarely need to change finance system. Often it is enough to connect the rest to it.

A new ERP system is a big step. Often the finance system works well, but orders, inventory and time live outside it and are typed in by hand. A connection sends the records automatically and removes the reconciliations.

Common connections

  • Orders and invoices to the finance system
  • Supplier invoices from the purchasing flow
  • Inventory value and costs per period
  • Import and export via API, CSV or Excel

Way of working

  • Feasibility study: which systems exist and what is typed twice
  • A clear quote before anything is built
  • A test environment where your finance lead approves before go-live

Frequently asked questions

Which finance systems can be connected?

Most modern finance systems have an API. Where there is none, file transfer is used.

What if the connection stops?

Errors are logged and raise a warning, so nothing disappears silently. Records can be resent once the error is fixed.
