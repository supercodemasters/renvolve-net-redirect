Services

Bookkeeping records that are right from the start

Built by someone who understands both the systems and the accounting.

Many systems create records that finance staff then fix by hand. I hold an ACCA qualification in accounting and have built finance modules with double-entry bookkeeping. So records can be coded correctly the moment the event happens.

What it can include

  • Chart of accounts and cost centres
  • General ledger and trial balance
  • Profit and loss and balance sheet
  • Export to your finance system or accounting firm

Relevant experience

  • Finance module with chart of accounts, double-entry and P&L (Samsara Solutions, Bulgaria)
  • ACCA Certified Accounting Technician (levels A and B)
  • Sync between QuickBooks and SugarCRM

No. It gives the firm better records, so they can spend time on advice instead of corrections.

Chart of accounts, VAT and periods are set up to your rules and your accounting firm’s requirements.
