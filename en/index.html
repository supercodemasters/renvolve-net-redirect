Parts of an ERP system

Start with the part where double work is biggest. The rest can follow.

  • Purchasing and suppliers

    Purchase orders, approval and suppliers in one flow.

  • Inventory management

    The right stock level in real time, from goods receipt to sale.

  • Orders and invoicing

    From quote to order, delivery and invoice.

  • Bookkeeping records and ledger

    Records that are right from the start, by double-entry.

  • Finance reports and KPIs

    Profit, margin and cash flow without a monthly puzzle.

  • Connect to your finance system

    Keep your finance system, connect the rest to it.

The whole picture

The ERP is the core, but it connects to the rest: booking, web shop, customer portals and dashboards. I also build integrations, websites and AI automation for businesses. All services are gathered on renvolve.biz.

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to replace our finance system?

Rarely. New parts are usually connected to the finance system you already have.

What does an ERP system cost?

It depends on how many parts are needed. After the study you get a written proposal with a price.

How long does it take?

A first module often takes a few weeks. Larger systems are built in steps over a few months.

Do you understand accounting?

Yes. I am an ACCA Certified Accounting Technician and have built finance modules with double-entry bookkeeping.
